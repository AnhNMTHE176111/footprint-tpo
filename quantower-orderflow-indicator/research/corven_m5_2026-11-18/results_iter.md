## 0. Calibration finding (changes earlier assumptions)

- The old axis `3792-(y-12.7)/14.0` is wrong. Fitting the 26 horizontal grid lines gives **price = 3792.8228 - 0.0723329*y** (13.825 px per 1.0, so 1 tick = 1.38 px, max residual 0.05). With it, candle HIGHS in the image equal our data to 0.00-0.03: **there is NO price offset between CORVEN's chart and our data** (the earlier '+0.2-0.3' came from the wrong scale). `calib.py` prints the check. Wick-bottom pixels read ~0.33 low (stroke + triangle), ignore.
- Old GT prices inside `fit_*.py` are therefore shifted by +0.1..+0.6 vs the new calibration. `gt_corven_m5.csv` supersedes them.
- A dot is ~9.5 px = ~7 ticks tall; circle radii are 9-22 px (6-16 ticks). Pixel precision is about +-1 tick (1 px = 0.7 tick).

## 2. Final rules (all per tick, no row grouping, baselines use only PREVIOUS bars)

Notation per bar and per traded tick p: bid, ask, side=max(bid,ask), weak=min(bid,ask), trades(p)=number of prints. B20_m = mean over the previous 20 bars of (volume per traded level of that bar). B20_mx = mean over the previous 20 bars of (max side over the levels of that bar). Colour = dominant side (ask>bid -> blue, else red/pink).

- **BIG circle** at tick p: `side(p) >= 0.5*B20_mx` AND side(p) is strictly the largest `side` within +-5 ticks of the same bar AND `side(p) >= 4*weak(p)` (weak floored at 1). Params: k=0.5, w=5 ticks, ratio=4.
- **DOT** at tick p: `trades(p) >= 2.4*B20_m` AND side(p) is strictly the largest `side` within +-3 ticks. Params: k=2.4, w=3.
- **EXHAUSTION** top (bottom) of a bar: `sum of Ask (Bid) over the 6 levels starting at the bar high (low) <= 1.1*B20_m` AND wick >= 3 ticks (wick = high - max(open,close), or min(open,close) - low). Params: n=6, k=1.1, wick=3.

Key findings behind the iterations:

- BIG: no single feature or small combination gets above 7-9 hits of 25 at +-2 ticks with 22-32 marks (random would give ~1-2). AUC of single cell features for 'cell is within 2 ticks of a CORVEN circle' is only 0.55-0.67; circle radius vs cell volume has Spearman 0.79 (circle SIZE is volume-driven, position is not tied to our local maxima). Colour = dominant side of the +-2-tick window agrees 22/25. A systematic vertical shift test (-8..+8 ticks) peaks at 0, so the position error is not a calibration problem. Untestable hypothesis: CORVEN's circles come from individual trade sizes (our `max_one_trade` column is 0).
- DOT: AUC of cell features for 'inside a CORVEN dot blob' is 0.85 (bid/side/vol/trades); `trades` beats `vol` consistently. Pixel F1 saturates at 0.45-0.49 even at 43-48 dots; at 33-37 dots it is 0.40-0.43. CORVEN shows no dots in 13:00-13:20 and 13:30-13:55 although those bars are loud; no bar-level gate explaining it was found.
- EXH: the summed soi-side volume over the 5-6 extreme levels has AUC 0.92-0.93 for 'CORVEN marked it' (smaller = marked). Recall 11/11 holds on a wide plateau (n=5: k 1.0-2.0, n=6: k 1.2-2.0, wick 2-3); only the number of extras changes (smoothly with k), so the law is not a lucky threshold.
